# Project Jal — Community Water Access

## Executive Summary
Safe drinking water points and community maintenance committees in water-stressed villages.

## Beneficiaries
Beneficiary count: 3,500 people
Groups: Rural Communities

## Geography
Rajasthan, Haryana

## Budget
Requested budget: ₹70 lakh
Duration: 20 months

## Objectives
- Commission functional community water points
- Train village water committees

## Risks
- Groundwater variability
- Permission delays

## Outcomes
- Functional water points
- Committee governance
