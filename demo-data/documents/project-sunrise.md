# Project Sunrise — Digital Learning for Rural Students

## Executive Summary
Project Sunrise aims to expand digital learning access for rural students across Haryana and Punjab through tablet labs, teacher enablement, and measurable learning outcomes.

## Need
Rural schools face limited digital infrastructure and uneven teacher readiness for blended learning models.

## Objectives
- Establish 40 digital learning labs in government schools
- Train 120 teachers in blended pedagogy
- Improve foundational literacy and numeracy outcomes

## Beneficiaries
Beneficiary count: 2,400 students
Groups: Students, Teachers, Rural Communities

## Geography
Haryana, Punjab

## Implementation Plan
Partner-led delivery with school cluster coordinators and quarterly learning assessments.

## Budget
Requested budget: ₹80 lakh
Duration: 18 months

## Timeline
### Milestones
- Lab setup and device deployment
- Teacher training cohorts
- Midline learning assessment
- Endline impact report

## Monitoring
Learning assessments at baseline, midline, and endline with attendance dashboards.

## Risks
- Device maintenance in remote schools
- Teacher attrition during training cycles
- Seasonal attendance dips

## Outcomes
- Measurable improvement in grade-level reading
- Sustained teacher digital competency
- 18-month sustainability plan with school ownership
