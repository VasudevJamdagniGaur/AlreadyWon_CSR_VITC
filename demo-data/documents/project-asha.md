# Project Asha — Maternal Healthcare Outreach

## Executive Summary
Community maternal healthcare outreach for underserved blocks in Rajasthan focusing on antenatal care, nutrition counseling, and safe delivery linkages.

## Need
High maternal risk in remote blocks with limited last-mile ANC coverage.

## Objectives
- Increase antenatal care coverage
- Reduce preventable pregnancy complications through early screening
- Strengthen ASHAs and local clinic referral pathways

## Beneficiaries
Beneficiary count: 1,800 women
Groups: Women, Rural Communities

## Geography
Rajasthan

## Budget
Requested budget: ₹60 lakh
Duration: 24 months

## Implementation Plan
Mobile clinics with community health workers and monthly camps.

## Milestones
- Community mapping and ASHA onboarding
- Mobile clinic launch
- Mid-program health indicators review
- Final outcome report

## Risks
- Local approval delays for mobile camps
- Seasonal access barriers
- Data quality for outcome measurement

## Outcomes
- Improved ANC visit completion
- Faster high-risk referral
