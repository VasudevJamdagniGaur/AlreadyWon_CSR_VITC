# Project Udaan — Youth Sports Development

## Executive Summary
Grassroots sports development for youth in Haryana communities with coaching, facilities access, and school-linked tournaments.

## Beneficiaries
Beneficiary count: 1,200 youth
Groups: Youth, Students

## Geography
Haryana, Delhi NCR

## Budget
Requested budget: ₹45 lakh
Duration: 12 months

## Objectives
- Expand access to structured sports coaching
- Build local coaching capacity
- Increase youth engagement and wellness

## Risks
- Facility scheduling conflicts
- Coach availability

## Outcomes
- Regular training participation
- Inter-school tournament pathway
